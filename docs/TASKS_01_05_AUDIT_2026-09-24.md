## Freshness: what the system can answer

Freshness has three separate meanings and must stay separate in reports:

1. integrity: whether downloaded bytes match the publisher checksum;
2. source age: source publication/replication time and its age at retrieval;
3. suitability: whether that age satisfies an agreed source-specific SLA.

The current report has the first two for Geofabrik and publication/retrieval
ages for Rosstat. `status=current` for Geofabrik means the local bytes match
the current publisher sidecar; it does not mean real-time data or a passed
age threshold. No age threshold has yet been approved, so suitability should
remain `unknown` until Task 10 sets one.

Geofabrik says its regional extracts are normally updated once daily, around
21:00 CET. Its 2026-09-24 release carried OSM replication through
2026-09-23T20:22:04Z and was about 14.5 hours old when checked. The standard
PBF has no past-edit history; Geofabrik also offers regional diffs and a
separate weekly history file. Public source documentation:
https://download.geofabrik.de/technical.html

A request-facing report should show, per field/source: `source_as_of` (only
when supplied), `published_at`, `retrieved_at`, age at retrieval and now,
checksum, source/schema version, access/license, and `current/stale/unknown`
against a documented SLA. Changes found by comparing snapshots are
`observed_between` dates, not registry event dates.

## What “full parcel information” should mean
A useful dossier is purpose-specific and presents coverage gaps explicitly.
It should combine:
- identity and cadastral geometry; area, category and permitted use;
- registered rights, restrictions, encumbrances and official source dates;
- planning/zoning rules and public-law zones that affect use;
- physical context: terrain, water, soils, environmental hazards and access;
- infrastructure proximity/capacity where the relevant network owner can
  lawfully provide it;
- valuation and market evidence, clearly distinguishing cadastral value,
  asking price and verified transaction data;
- dated history of source observations, field-level provenance and conflicts.

No single open source is shown to provide all of this. “Complete” should be a
coverage matrix of known, unknown, unavailable, restricted and not-applicable
facts; it must never be a blanket guarantee for every parcel.

## Source integration feasibility

| Layer | Likely route | Integration status / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| EGRN object characteristics, rights, restrictions | Official EGRN electronic request/extract; validate signed response and allowed automation. | Official request service and extract types exist. A documented public bulk API/event feed was not confirmed in this audit. Owner personal data is restricted; obtain only via authorized route/consent. |
| Public cadastral geometry/map | NSPD public map and any officially documented service endpoint. | Public map availability does not by itself establish bulk API rights, quotas or stable schema. Task 13 must verify endpoint and terms before automation. |
| Rights-transition history | Separate official extract on transfer of rights. | Useful for registered right transitions, not a complete event stream of every parcel attribute edit. |
| Planning and land-use constraints | Regional/municipal GISOGD, PZZ, general plans, official documents and OGC services where offered. | Technically integratable per publisher; coverage, versions, API and reuse terms vary by region. |
| Roads and place context | Geofabrik/OSM, already integrated by Task 5. | Good for mapped context; not proof of legal access, road ownership or current physical condition. |
| Cadastral valuation/statistics | Official public datasets and valuation authority publications. | Integrate when a stable file/API and keys are confirmed; value is not market sale price. |
| Utilities / engineering capacity | Requests or agreements with grid, water, gas and telecom operators; partner APIs where offered. | No universal open source; restricted technical conditions require explicit authorization. |
| Terrain, water and environmental context | Official geospatial services and licensed/open satellite or elevation sources. | Feasible as separate layers; imagery requires interpretation and does not establish legal status. |
| Market activity | Public auction portals, lawful licensed providers, or a sourced observation database. | Listings are asking prices; verified registered transaction prices may not be openly bulk-accessible. |

Rosreestr lists multiple EGRN extract types, including object-characteristic
and transfer-of-rights extracts. Its current guidance says owner personal
data in third-party extracts depends on the owner opening those details.
Official references: https://rosreestr.gov.ru/eservices/request_info_from_egrn/
and https://rosreestr.gov.ru/press/news/501879/ and
https://rosreestr.gov.ru/press/news/428759/

   verify the GitHub Actions result after pushing this commit. Keep the
   full PBF download gate manual.

   counts while retaining raw events; benchmark extraction and SQLite writes;
   preserve fail-closed geometry/CRS/natural-key checks.
3. **Task 1 and Task 10 freshness:** define source-specific age thresholds
   with the user; display checksum integrity separately from age suitability.
   Evaluate applying Geofabrik regional diffs (kept for 100 days) with a full
   PBF fallback, then compare resulting data against a fresh publisher release.
4. **Tasks 6–8:** validate OKTMO hierarchy/history, then cross-layer keys,   endpoint types and evidence references; do not infer unsupported links.
5. **Task 13 discovery gate:** before connector code, test the official NSPD/
   EGRN route, authentication, allowed automation, costs/quotas, fields,
   geometry, timestamps, update semantics and privacy using several test
   cadastral numbers. If there is no permitted stable API, use an authorized
   official extract workflow or a contracted provider adapter; do not scrape
   an undocumented UI.
6. **Task 13 implementation:** preserve signed/raw snapshots and hashes,
   normalize only source-backed facts into `ParcelRecord`, store multiple
   observations, and report differences as observed changes unless the source
   gives an effective/event date.
7. **Later layers:** add planning restrictions, public valuation, utilities,
   terrain/environment and market data as separate adapters. Every output
   field must carry source, date, access status and quality state.
